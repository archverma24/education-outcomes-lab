# Day 11 — 2026-10-07

## Added

The school-level summary now includes the range of historical final grades, defined as the highest minus the lowest grade in each school. The dashboard offers a CSV download of exactly the displayed aggregate table: school, course-record count, mean, median, and range.

## Why

A portable summary helps users examine the descriptive results outside the dashboard. Keeping the CSV tied to the displayed table prevents a mismatch and avoids exporting student-level rows.

## Limitation and learning

Range is sensitive to one extreme record; it describes spread but does not measure uncertainty or explain why grades differ. The summaries do not rank schools or support decisions about current students. Day 10's verified PR #11 merge is reconciled in this PR.
