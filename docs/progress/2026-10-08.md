# Day 12 — 2026-10-08

## Added

The dashboard now shows a final-grade outlier inspection chart with counts below, within, and above 1.5×IQR fences. It states the quartiles and numerical cutoffs for all Portuguese-course records.

## Why

A visible descriptive rule makes unusually low or high grades easier to inspect without silently treating them as errors. All source rows remain in the dataset and model evaluation.

## Limitation and learning

The 1.5×IQR rule is descriptive and can be sensitive to the dataset's grade distribution. A flagged value is not necessarily a mistake, and this view does not justify excluding a student or making a decision about current students. Day 11's verified PR #12 merge is reconciled in this PR.
