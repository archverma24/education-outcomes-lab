# Day 19 — 2026-10-15

## Added

The CLI now exports repeated five-fold validation results from the outer training partition. Each fold records counts, validation row positions, and MAE/RMSE for linear regression and a fold-training mean baseline.

## Why

This completes the delayed Day 17 prerequisite before uncertainty summaries and residual diagnostics. The reserved test portion is excluded from model fitting and fold scoring.

## Limitation and learning

Repeated folds use the same historical training records, so fold scores are correlated and do not establish real-world uncertainty. The Day 1 holdout was already viewed and remains separate from model selection. Row positions are meaningful only for the source hash recorded in the report.
