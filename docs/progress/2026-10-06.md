# Day 10 — 2026-10-06

## Added

The dashboard shows an alphabetical table of school-level final-grade mean, median, and course-record count. The view describes historical Portuguese-course records without ranking schools or claiming a school caused a grade difference.

## Why

A mean alone hides how many records support it. Showing sample sizes and medians makes the comparison easier to interpret without presenting it as a school-performance judgment.
